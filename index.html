Legislation Phases
⚖Hazards in Social Housing (Prescribed Requirements) (England) Regulations 2025 — Social Housing (Regulation) Act 2023.
1
✅ Live — 27 October 2025
Damp, Mould & Emergency Hazards
Emergency hazards (broken boilers, gas leaks, CO) — investigate & make safe within 24 hours
Significant damp & mould — investigate within 10 working days
Written findings to tenant within 3 working days of investigation concluding
Make safe within 5 working days of investigation concluding
Applies to social housing tenancies in England
2
🟡 Phase 2 — 2026 (date TBC)
Expanded Hazards
Excess cold and excess heat
Falls — bathrooms, stairs, between levels
Structural collapse and explosions
Fire and electrical hazards
Domestic and personal hygiene, food safety
3
🟠 Phase 3 — 2027
All HHSRS Hazards
All 29 HHSRS hazard categories presenting significant risk of harm
Excluding overcrowding
Private rented sector — Renters' Rights Bill (TBC)
Who Is Covered
✅Social housing tenancies — housing associations
✅Local authority / council housing
✅Registered providers of social housing
🔄Private rented sector — Renters' Rights Bill (TBC)
✗Shared ownership — excluded
✗Long leaseholds — excluded
✗Temporary / supported accommodation — excluded
✗Scotland — separate legislation in development
Non-Compliance Consequences
⚖Landlords face court action for breach of contract, unlimited fines, Housing Ombudsman findings, Regulator of Social Housing escalation and tenant compensation claims. Lifestyle is no longer a valid defence for damp and mould.
